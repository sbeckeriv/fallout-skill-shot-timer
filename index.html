When to shoot the ball

Launch inside the blue window and the ball rolls over the light while it is ON. Change any timing below and everything updates.

Settings

Ball travel is the time from launch until the ball reaches the light. Reaction is how long it takes you to press after you see the light change; about 0.2–0.25 s is typical. Settings are saved in this browser.
