Fallout skill shot timer

When to shoot the ball

The two inlane lights alternate. Launch inside the blue window and the ball rolls over the right inlane while it is ON. The page picks the easiest light change to time your shot from. Change any timing below and everything updates.

Settings

Ball travel is the time from launch until the ball reaches the right inlane light. Reaction is how long it takes you to press after you see a light change; about 0.2–0.25 s is typical. Settings are saved in this browser.
